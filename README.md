# Ashtanga Primary Series Flashcards

A single-page flashcard app for learning the Ashtanga Primary Series (Yoga Chikitsa) and preparing for a yoga teacher training midterm. Open `index.html` in a browser. There is no build step and nothing to install.

## Asanas tab (73 cards)
- Sanskrit and English names, pronunciation, drishti (gaze point), breath count, a breath-led cue and a line-art illustration
- Three tags you can filter by: pose family, spinal action, and energy (warming / cooling / neutral)
- Two guides: **How to sequence** and **Cue by family**
- Flip cards, quiz English-first or Sanskrit-first, shuffle, and mark cards as learned

## Exam Prep tab (70 cards)
- Ten topics in the order of the study guide, with progress per topic
- Card types that mirror the exam: definitions, multiple choice, matching, "circle all", Sanskrit translations and write-your-own prompts
- Weak spots, a 20-question mock exam, search and a 14-day study plan
- Answers were drafted from the course materials and checked against them. Items marked "verify" are not confirmed by those materials and should be checked against your own class notes.

Progress is saved in your browser on your own device.

## Run it
- **Locally:** open `index.html`. Keep the `assets/` folder next to it.
- **On GitHub Pages:** Settings > Pages, "Deploy from a branch", `main` / root. It is served at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

The page loads two fonts (Montserrat, Open Sans) from Google Fonts and falls back to system fonts offline.

## Files
- `index.html`: the whole app (HTML, CSS and JS)
- `assets/poses/ai/`: one illustration per pose. Four newer poses (Vrksasana, Natarajasana, Parivritta Utkatasana, Malasana) show an "image coming soon" placeholder.

## Illustrations
The pose illustrations were AI-generated for this project in one consistent style, so there are no third-party image credits. AI can get yoga anatomy wrong, so please check poses with a qualified teacher before teaching from them.
